Hi team,

Five weeks of picks. Bragging rights won and lost. At least one shootout that nearly gave the office a heart attack. World Cup 2026 is officially a wrap — and it delivered.
Spain beat Argentina 1–0 after extra time to lift their second World Cup — won exactly like their first, sixteen years on: 1–0 in extra time. It went down to the wire: goalless through 90 minutes, one moment of Spanish magic, and Messi’s last World Cup dream ending inches short. And what a tournament it took to get there — minnows Cape Verde held Spain to a draw, and Germany and the Netherlands both crashed out on penalties in the Round of 32.
